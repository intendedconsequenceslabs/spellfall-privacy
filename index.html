Spellfall Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Overview

Spellfall does not require an account. Game progress, settings, and lifetime statistics are stored on your device. Spellfall uses Apple and third-party services for advertising, App Store purchases, and Game Center. Word definitions use Apple's on-device system dictionary. Sound effects and music are included in the app and play on your device.

Advertising

Spellfall uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform to show between-game and optional rewarded ads, manage consent, limit fraud, and measure ad performance. Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, Google may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, advertising data, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. You can revisit available consent choices through Manage Ad Privacy in Spellfall Settings.

Google describes its practices in the Google Privacy Policy. Spellfall requires its service providers to protect data consistently with this policy, applicable law, and Apple's App Review requirements.

Purchases

Spellfall Premium is processed by Apple through the App Store. Spellfall checks Apple-verified purchase entitlements to unlock Premium but does not receive your payment-card details.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, Spellfall sends scores, leaderboard records, and completed achievements to Apple. Game Center is optional and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and your Apple Account settings.

Word definitions

When you choose to look up a word, Spellfall uses Apple's on-device system dictionary. Spellfall does not send the word to its own server or to a third-party dictionary service. Dictionary availability and content are controlled by iOS and the dictionaries installed on your device.

Sound and music

Spellfall's sound effects and background music are included in the app and play entirely on your device. Spellfall does not stream audio, does not record audio, and never accesses the microphone. Volume levels and sound choices are settings stored on your device. To avoid interrupting you, Spellfall checks whether other audio is already playing when it starts — if you are listening to your own music or a podcast, Spellfall's music stays silent. Sound follows your device's ring/silent switch.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete Spellfall. Apple controls retention of App Store and Game Center records. Google controls retention of advertising and consent records under its policies. Spellfall does not operate an account database containing player profiles. To remove local data, delete the app. You may manage Game Center through your Apple Account, and available advertising choices and music and sound levels through Spellfall Settings.

Children

Spellfall is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. Spellfall does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Spellfall's features or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or to report an inappropriate ad, email spellfallgamesupport@gmail.com.
